# AI Transparency Charter – Projects built with AI assistance

October 17, 2026 · Zayla ([@Zeusfail](https://github.com/Zeusfail))

> 🇫🇷 *Version française : [AI-CHARTER.fr.md](AI-CHARTER.fr.md)*

I clearly flag every project where I used artificial intelligence, so you know what was produced, how, and who answers for it.

This charter sets out my commitments to the people who use, read or fund my projects. It applies to every project listed in the register below. I'm Zayla (Zeusfail on GitHub), I make games, mods and tools, and I remain solely responsible for everything I publish, whatever tool I used.

## My commitments

I commit to seven concrete points.

1. **Transparency**: I disclose AI use on every project concerned, visibly and without having to be asked.
2. **Accuracy**: I state which tools I used (name, and version when I know it) and which part of the work was handed to AI.
3. **Human responsibility**: I review, understand and approve everything I publish. A mistake made by AI is still my mistake.
4. **Verification**: I test the code, check facts and figures, and don't publish anything I haven't checked.
5. **Sources and rights**: I credit my sources, respect licenses and copyright, and never use AI to imitate someone's work or voice without their consent.
6. **Personal data**: I never send confidential or personal third-party data to an AI tool without a legal basis or consent. For health data (such as medical images), I only use anonymized data and I state which tool had access to it.
7. **Corrections**: if a problem is reported to me, I reply within 7 days and publicly correct whatever needs correcting.

## AI assistance levels

Each project gets a level from 0 to 5, so disclosures can be compared from one project to another.

| Level | Name | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | No AI | No generative AI tool used. | Text and code written entirely by me |
| 1 | Advice and research | AI explained things, informed me or gave me ideas. It produced nothing that is published. | Questions about a concept, brainstorming, research help |
| 2 | Correction and improvement | AI reviewed, corrected or rephrased content I had already written. | Spell-checking, debugging my own code, rephrasing |
| 3 | Co-creation | AI produced a significant part of the content or code, which I directed, reviewed and adapted. | Structure and code generated, then modified and tested by me |
| 4 | Directed generation | Most of the work was produced by AI from my detailed instructions, with full review and approval on my side. | Page or tool generated almost entirely, checked before release |
| 5 | Autonomous production | AI worked autonomously across several steps (agent, automation), with my oversight only at the start and the end. | Project built by an agent, then audited and tested by me |

The level measures how much of the work AI produced, not how thoroughly I checked it: that is described in the "Done by me" and "Verification" columns of the register. If I rewrite a large part of what AI produced, I lower the level accordingly. When different parts of a project fall under different levels, I give the highest one and describe the rest in the "Done by AI" column.
